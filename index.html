Walyink Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your privacy matters. Walyink is designed as an offline-first utility with minimal permissions and zero tracking.

Overview

Walyink is a WhatsApp link and QR generator that works primarily offline. The application does not require registration, login, or cloud services. All generated data remains stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

Walyink does not collect, store, or share any personal information. No analytics, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs are used.

Local Device Storage

All saved links and history are stored locally on your device only. Data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

Permissions

• Internet: Used only for opening WhatsApp links (wa.me)
• Media Access: Used only when saving QR images to your gallery

No access to contacts, camera, location, or microphone is requested.

QR Code Generation

QR codes are generated completely offline on your device. No data is uploaded or processed on external servers.

Sharing

Shared content is handled by the system share sheet only. Walyink does not track or store shared data.

Third-Party Services

Walyink uses WhatsApp wa.me links provided by Meta. The app is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Security

The app follows modern Android security practices including minimal permissions, local storage only, and no network-based data collection.

Children’s Privacy

Walyink does not target children under 13 and does not collect any data from minors.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Continued use of the app means acceptance of any changes.
