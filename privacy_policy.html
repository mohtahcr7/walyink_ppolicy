Walyink – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Overview

Walyink is a 100% offline tool. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All saved links are stored locally on your device only.

Data We Collect

None. We do not collect personal information, analytics, crash reports, or usage data of any kind.

Permissions

Storage permission is requested only when you choose to save a QR code image to your device gallery. No camera, contacts, or location access is requested.

Third-Party Services

Walyink generates standard wa.me links per WhatsApp's official developer documentation. Using these links subjects you to WhatsApp/Meta Terms of Service. We have no affiliation with Meta or WhatsApp Inc.

Children's Privacy

This app does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.
